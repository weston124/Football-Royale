FOOTBALL ROYALE

Privacy

Plain-language version. Last updated November 2026.

The short version
  • 🔒 We keep your email only as a scrambled code (a one-way hash). Nobody can read it, not even the admin.
  • 🔑 Your password is hashed too. Nobody can see it.
  • 👀 Your username is public: other players see it in matches. Don't use your real name.
  • 🚫 No ads. No selling or sharing your data. No tracking or analytics companies.
  • 💬 There is no chat with other players.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Under 13? You need a parent's or guardian's OK before you sign up.
  • 🗑️ You can delete your account any time (see below).

What we keep

WhatWhyWho can see it
UsernameSo friends can find you and so you show up in matchesEveryone who plays
Email, as a one-way hashTo log in with your email and to stop two accounts using one emailNobody (it can't be turned back into your email)
Password, as a one-way hashTo check your password when you log inNobody
Ball Bucks, items you own, wins, eliminations, matches played, the date you joinedTo run the gameYou; the game's admin sees usernames and game numbers only
A sign-in code saved in your browserSo you stay logged in for up to 30 daysOnly your browser
Your outfit and settings, saved in your browserSo your player looks the same next timeOnly your browser (and other players see your outfit in a match)

We use your internet address (IP address) only for a short time in memory to stop spam and password guessing. We don't save it with your account. The company that hosts our server may keep normal server logs for a short time for security.

We don't use cookies for ads or tracking. We don't ask for your real name, address, phone number, photo, voice or location.

Kids and parents

Football Royale is made for players about 10 to 14. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must say it's OK before you make an account. The sign-up checkbox asks you to confirm this.

Parents: we collect as little as possible (see the table above). Your child's email is stored only as a hash, so we can't email them or read it. You can ask us to delete your child's account at any time: use the form below with their username and password, or contact the game's owner with their username. If we learn that a child under 13 signed up without a parent's OK, we will delete the account.

Delete your account

This deletes your username, Ball Bucks, items and stats forever. It can't be undone.

Forgot your password? Contact the game's owner with your username and we will help.

Changes

If we change this page in a big way, we'll post it in the game's Updates box first.
